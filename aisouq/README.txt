- assets/style.css        Custom styling and responsive layouts

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP while keeping the folder structure intact.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the files to your hosting/web root.
3. Bootstrap, Font Awesome and Google Fonts are loaded from CDNs, so an internet connection is needed for those resources.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
- The enquiry forms are front-end demonstrations. They open the visitor's default email app with a pre-filled message addressed to info@aisouq.ae. They do not securely store or send submissions directly. Connect a backend, CRM, or approved form service before production.
- The quote popup is set to appear after a short delay once per browser session. All “Get a Free Quote” buttons also open it.
- Review all business details, regulatory claims, social media links, privacy wording and service coverage before publishing. SIRA/ADMCC approval wording should only be used where it accurately applies.
- Replace the placeholder social profile URLs with AI Souq's official social links.
