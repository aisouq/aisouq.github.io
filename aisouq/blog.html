SECURITY • 5 MIN READ

Choosing the right CCTV system for your property

The right surveillance system begins with understanding what you need to see, where cameras should be placed and how footage will be reviewed. Consider lighting conditions, indoor or outdoor placement, recording retention, remote access and how the system may grow over time.

Start with the coverage plan

Map key entrances, circulation areas and locations where monitoring is most useful. Avoid relying on camera count alone: a well-positioned camera can be more useful than several poorly placed units.

Think beyond the camera

Recording equipment, network capacity, storage, secure access and maintenance all matter. Ask your installer to explain the system clearly and confirm any local regulatory requirements that apply to your property.

CONNECTIVITY GUIDE
NETWORKING • 4 MIN READ

How to improve Wi-Fi coverage at home or work

Wireless performance depends on more than your internet package. Building materials, interference, router placement, connected devices and the size of your space can all affect coverage.

Place access points thoughtfully

Keep wireless equipment in suitable central locations where possible, and consider dedicated access points for larger villas, multi-floor properties or busy workplaces. A site assessment can help identify weak spots before equipment is installed.

Plan for the way you use the network

Video calls, streaming, cloud applications and guest devices place different demands on a network. A properly planned solution balances coverage, capacity and security.

BUSINESS SECURITY
ACCESS CONTROL • 4 MIN READ

Is access control right for your business?

Access control helps organisations manage who can enter selected spaces. Depending on your needs, systems may use cards, PINs, biometrics or other credentials, with different permissions for staff, visitors and restricted areas.

Match the system to your workflow

Consider how many doors need protection, how permissions are managed, whether entry records are needed and what happens when a credential is lost or a team member leaves.

Plan for integration and support

Think about power, network connectivity, door hardware, user training and future expansion. The right design should fit your security requirements without making everyday access unnecessarily complicated.
