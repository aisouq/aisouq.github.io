Intercom and Cabling Services

Residential Intercom Solutions

Audio and video intercom systems for villas, gated communities, apartments and residential compounds, with visitor verification and compatible mobile-access options.

Commercial Intercom Systems

Communication systems for offices, retail outlets, hotels, educational institutions and healthcare facilities, supporting visitor management and internal communication.

Structured Cabling Installation

Organized voice, data, CCTV, access-control and networking cabling designed to support reliable connectivity and future expansion.

Network and Communication Infrastructure

Cat6, Cat6A and Cat7 cabling, fibre-optic cabling, network racks, patch panels, data outlets and communication cabinets.

Solutions for Different Properties

  • Villas and residential developments: intercoms, smart door entry and network cabling.
  • Corporate offices and business centres: structured cabling and communication systems for offices and headquarters.
  • Hotels and hospitality: guest communication, reception connectivity and room-to-room intercom options.
  • Warehouses and industrial facilities: network cabling, fibre infrastructure and communication systems.

Installation Process

  • 1. Site survey: assess communication requirements, network capacity and expansion plans.
  • 2. Infrastructure design: prepare cable routes, equipment recommendations, timeline and proposal.
  • 3. Professional installation: install cabling, intercoms, racks and communication components.
  • 4. Configuration and testing: test communication points, network connectivity, video quality and system performance.
  • 5. Training and handover: provide user guidance and available project documentation, such as network diagrams.
  • 6. Maintenance and support: arrange troubleshooting, upgrades and preventive maintenance as agreed.
