Solutions for Different Meeting Spaces

  • Huddle rooms: compact video conferencing and display solutions for small teams.
  • Conference rooms and boardrooms: audio, video and presentation equipment planned around room size and seating.
  • Training rooms and classrooms: displays, microphones and collaboration tools for workshops and learning.
  • Executive meeting rooms: professional display, conferencing and room-control options.
  • Multi-room offices: consistent technology across rooms, with configurations matched to each space.

Installation Process

  • 1. Requirements and site assessment: review room size, seating, existing equipment and preferred meeting platforms.
  • 2. Solution design and quotation: define displays, cameras, microphones, speakers and project scope.
  • 3. Installation and configuration: install and configure agreed equipment and connections when the site is ready.
  • 4. Testing and commissioning: test audio, video and platform connectivity with compatible equipment.
  • 5. Training and handover: provide basic user guidance and demonstrate the installed system.
  • 6. Ongoing support: arrange troubleshooting or maintenance under agreed terms.

Meeting Room Installation Service

AI Souq lists installation-only options for meeting rooms accommodating up to 6 or up to 10 participants. The service covers installation and basic setup of client-supplied equipment, audio and video checks, platform connectivity testing and basic user training.

Equipment, accessories and platform licences must be supplied by the client or purchased separately. Network infrastructure installation, civil and electrical work, and display wall-mounting structures are excluded from the listed service. Additional work may be quoted separately.
