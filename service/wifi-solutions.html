ONGOING SUPPORT

Wi-Fi Maintenance Service.

Keep your wireless network under review with visit-based maintenance options for eligible systems.

The listed Wi-Fi maintenance service offers options for up to 10 or 50 devices. Each package includes one onsite maintenance visit and one remote support session to be used within 30 days of the visit. This is a per-visit service, not a fixed annual contract.

Maintenance can include

  • Equipment inspection and a basic system health check
  • Coverage and connectivity assessment
  • Basic troubleshooting and configuration adjustments
  • Recommendations for suitable improvements

Replacement parts, new cabling and internet service provider issues are excluded. Additional work can be quoted separately. Confirm the exact package scope when requesting a quotation.
