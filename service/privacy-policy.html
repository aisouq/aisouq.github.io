Who we are

AiSouq is a business based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. In this policy, “AiSouq”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean AiSouq.

This policy applies to our websites, aisouq.ae and aisouquae.com, and to enquiries you send us through our forms, phone, email, WhatsApp and online advertisements. We handle personal data in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and its related regulations.

What we collect

The data we collect depends on how you use our websites and services.

When youWe may collect
Send an enquiry, request a quote or book a free site surveyName, phone number, email address, the service you need, your project details, site location and any message you write.
Message or call usYour WhatsApp number and profile name, the content of your messages, call details and any files or photos you send.
Buy from our shopName, contact details, delivery address, order details and payment confirmation. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are not stored by us.
Use an AMC, IT support or installation serviceBusiness or property details, site and equipment information, contact people and service history.
Visit our websitesDevice and browser type, pages viewed, approximate location, the website or advertisement that brought you to us, and how you interact with the pages.

Please do not send us sensitive information, such as ID numbers or passwords, unless we ask for it for a specific reason.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange a site survey.
  • To supply, install, maintain and support products and services, and to process orders and deliveries.
  • To manage contracts, invoices and customer records.
  • To follow up on your enquiry and, where you agree or the law allows, to tell you about relevant offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To understand how our websites and advertisements perform, so we can improve them.
  • To prevent fraud and spam, keep our systems secure, and meet legal obligations.

WhatsApp, forms and lead forms

Website enquiry form. When you fill in our enquiry form, the form prepares a WhatsApp message with your details. Nothing is sent to us until you tap Send in WhatsApp. Once you send it, WhatsApp's own privacy terms also apply to the message.

Google Ads lead forms. If you submit a lead form shown in a Google advertisement, Google receives your details first and passes them to us. Google's privacy policy applies to how Google handles that data.

Calls and messages. We may keep a record of your enquiry in our customer or lead records so that our team can follow up and avoid asking you the same questions twice.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to make our websites work and to understand how they are used. These include:

  • Google Analytics to measure visits, pages viewed and enquiries.
  • Google Tag Manager to manage our measurement tags.
  • Google Ads to measure whether an advertisement led to an enquiry and to show relevant advertisements.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the websites may not work properly without them. You can also opt out of Google's advertising personalisation through your Google account settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where needed:

  • Our team and contractors who help us handle enquiries, deliveries, installations, support, website and marketing work. They must keep your data confidential and use it only for our purposes.
  • Service providers such as website hosting, email, messaging, analytics, advertising and payment providers, including Google and Meta (WhatsApp).
  • Delivery and installation partners who need your name, contact number and address to reach you.
  • Authorities and advisers where the law requires it, or where we need to protect our rights.

Data processed outside the UAE

Some of the providers we use, including Google and Meta, may process data on servers outside the UAE. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data is protected in line with applicable law.

How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, such as handling your enquiry, delivering your service, meeting accounting and legal requirements, or resolving disputes. When we no longer need it, we delete it or anonymise it.

How we protect data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including restricted access to customer records and secure systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to UAE law, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data or restrict how we use it.
  • Stop using your data for marketing.
  • Withdraw your consent where we rely on it. This will not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email info@aisouq.ae. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also contact the UAE Data Office.

Children

Our websites and services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time to see the latest version.
