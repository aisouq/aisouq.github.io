Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a virtual IT department?

A virtual IT department gives your business access to outsourced technical assistance under an agreed support plan, including remote and onsite services specified in your package.

Which package should I choose?

Compare your employee count and required support allowances. The plans cover up to 10, 30 or 50 employees respectively.

Are onsite visits included?

Yes. Each plan includes scheduled and emergency visits with different allowances. See the package details and agreement for the applicable terms.

Which systems are supported?

Depending on your plan, support can include computers, software, networks, Wi-Fi, CCTV, meeting rooms, access control and time attendance.

Where is the service available?

The packages are offered for businesses in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, subject to the agreed service terms.

What happens if we need more support than the plan includes?

Contact AiSouq to discuss additional support and any applicable charges before extra work is carried out.
