What the Installation Service Includes

The listed installation service covers client-supplied equipment and includes:

  • PABX or IP PBX system and handset installation.
  • Basic extension programming and configuration.
  • Installation of standard system accessories.
  • Incoming, outgoing and internal call testing.
  • System commissioning, basic user guidance and handover.

Exclusions: structured cabling, civil and electrical works, SIP trunk provisioning, internet service, equipment, licences, gateways and switches are excluded and may require a separate quotation. Site readiness and service terms apply.

Features to Discuss

System capabilities depend on the selected equipment, licensing, telecom service and agreed configuration. Discuss the features you need when requesting a quotation.

  • Extension calling: connect staff through internal extensions.
  • Call routing and auto attendant: direct incoming calls to the appropriate team where supported.
  • Voicemail and call handling: select supported features to suit your business.
  • Remote user support: discuss compatible remote extensions and connectivity requirements.
