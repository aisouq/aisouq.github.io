01 / CCTV MAINTENANCE

CCTV AMC in Dubai & UAE

Dirty or misaligned cameras, recording faults and storage issues can leave gaps in footage. A CCTV maintenance contract helps identify problems before they affect day-to-day security.

  • Camera cleaning and view checks
  • Recorder and storage health checks
  • Firmware/software updates where supported
  • Cabling, power and connector inspection
  • Remote viewing and app-access checks
  • Fault handling and service reporting as agreed

Suitable for: offices, warehouses, shops, villas and other sites with CCTV systems. Equipment compatibility and any initial survey requirements will be confirmed before acceptance.

03 / ACCESS CONTROL

Access Control AMC

Doors, gates and turnstiles rely on readers, controllers, locks and power supplies working correctly. Maintenance helps keep access systems dependable and user permissions current.

  • Card, fingerprint or face-reader checks
  • Controller, lock and power-supply inspection
  • Door and gate alarm tests where applicable
  • User and permission review by authorised staff
  • Software and backup checks where supported
