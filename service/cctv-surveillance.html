YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in the UAE?

The cost depends on the number and type of cameras, recorder and storage requirements, cabling distance and property size. We provide a tailored quotation after reviewing your needs.

Do I need SIRA or ADMCC approval for CCTV?

Security system requirements differ between Dubai, Abu Dhabi and other emirates, and may depend on the property type. Confirm the applicable SIRA, ADMCC or local authority requirement during project planning.

How long does CCTV installation take?

Installation time depends on property size, camera quantity, cabling and site readiness. A project timeline is provided with your quotation.

Can I view CCTV cameras on my mobile phone?

Yes. Compatible CCTV systems can be configured for secure live and recorded viewing through a smartphone or computer.

Do you offer CCTV maintenance?

Yes. CCTV support and maintenance contracts can be included as an optional service.

Which UAE areas do you cover?

Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

Choose the Right CCTV Technology for Your Site

The right camera system is about more than the number of cameras. We help you consider image clarity, lighting, coverage, recording time, connectivity and how footage will be accessed by authorised users.

Day & Night Coverage

Discuss camera placement, low-light performance and night-vision options for entrances, corridors, parking areas and outdoor spaces.

Recording & Storage

Plan recorder capacity and storage around camera count, resolution, recording schedules and the retention period you require.

Secure Remote Access

Set up compatible mobile or desktop viewing and review access settings so authorised people can check the system conveniently.

System Expansion

Consider future camera additions, network capacity and recorder compatibility when planning a new installation or upgrade.

PLAN BEFORE INSTALLATION

What We Review During a CCTV Site Survey

A site survey helps turn your security goals into a practical system plan. We review the property, discuss priority areas and identify installation considerations before preparing a quotation.

Request Your Site Survey
  • Coverage priorities: entrances, exits, reception areas, cash points, stock rooms, parking and perimeter zones.
  • Camera positioning: suitable mounting locations, viewing angles, lighting conditions and potential obstructions.
  • Cabling and connectivity: cable routes, network availability, power requirements and equipment locations.
  • Recording requirements: expected retention, recorder placement, storage capacity and user access needs.
  • Compliance considerations: identify applicable authority or property requirements for confirmation before implementation.
  • Next steps: recommended system scope, installation approach and a quotation based on the agreed requirements.
